Flash the CareLink home display

Puts the released firmware on an M5Stack Core Ink, from this page, over USB. Nothing to install.

This browser cannot do it. Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop; phones, Firefox and Safari have no access to USB serial ports. This page must be opened over https.

Steps

  1. Connect the Core Ink to the computer with a USB-C data cable. A charge-only cable shows no port.
  2. Press the button above and pick the serial port (often named USB Serial or CH9102). No port in the list: try another cable, then install the CH9102 driver from M5Stack.
  3. Choose Install, and answer the question about erasing (below).
  4. Wait for it to finish, about a minute. Unplug, then power the display from a proper USB adapter.

Erase or not?

You areChooseResult
staging a new set, or moving a display to another householdEraseAll settings are gone, the WiFi password included. The display starts in setup mode and shows a QR code.
updating a display that is in useDo not eraseWiFi and proxy settings are kept. The display carries on with the new firmware.
This display repeats information from an insulin pump. It is not a medical device and not an alarm system. After flashing, watch it show a current reading, with the right time, before relying on it.
